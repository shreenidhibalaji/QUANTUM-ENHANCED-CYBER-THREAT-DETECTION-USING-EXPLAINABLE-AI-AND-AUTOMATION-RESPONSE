Understanding SHAP Values

What are SHAP values?

SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) values explain individual predictions by quantifying the contribution of each feature.

  • Positive values (red): Push prediction towards "attack"
  • Negative values (blue): Push prediction towards "normal"
  • Larger absolute values: Stronger influence on the decision

How to interpret the plots:

  • Feature Importance: Shows which features matter most overall
  • Summary Plot: Shows the distribution of feature impacts
  • Waterfall Plots: Break down individual predictions step by step
  • Force Plot: Visualizes the "forces" pushing the prediction
